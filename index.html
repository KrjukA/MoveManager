Features

Everything around the Move, in one place.

Free and open source. Runs on your computer and talks to Move over your Wi-Fi.

Projects 01

  • Pads in the same 4×8 layout as on Move
  • Drag projects to Move and back, swap pads
  • Copy a project on Move with Option/Alt-drag
  • Groups, descriptions and notes on the computer

Key & tempo 02

  • Key, scale and tempo of every project
  • Camelot codes to plan a live set
  • Change key, tempo and name in place

Listening 03

  • Each saved project gets a WAV or MP3 demo
  • Monitor an input, e.g. Move over USB
  • Waveform while previewing samples

Inside a project 04

  • Reorder tracks
  • Load and export MIDI clips
  • Copy, cut, paste and swap clips
  • Audio clips in audio tracks

Drum generator 05

  • Patterns in the style of many genres
  • Learns from your own MIDI clips
  • Mutation, ghost notes, euclid, half time
  • Listen before it lands in a cell

Samples & presets 06

  • Copy both ways between computer and Move
  • Rename and move — projects keep their sound
  • Find samples no project uses
  • Schwung slots are taken into account

History 07

  • Last 20 versions of every project
  • Go back to any version — and undo that too

Safe 08

  • Own SSH key, accepted on Move with the wheel
  • Deleted items go to the Trash
  • Runs on your computer, nothing in the cloud

Comfortable 09

  • Dark and light themes
  • English and Russian
  • Tells you when a new version is out and installs it

Windows

Windows 10 and 11.

Download
1 · UnzipOn a Mac, drag the app into Applications. On Windows, Extract All.
2 · First launchThe app has no paid signature. Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Windows: More info → Run anyway.
3 · Connect MoveSame Wi-Fi as the computer. Enter the code Move shows on its screen — once.

Step-by-step guides in English and Russian are on the release page. Mac says it “can't be opened”? Paste this line into Terminal and press Enter:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/KRJUK Move Manager.app"
